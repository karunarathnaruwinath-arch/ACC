SRI RAHULA M.M.V. — GATE OPENING WELCOME PORTAL

This version uses the supplied school-gate photograph as the opening scene.

Opening sequence:
1. School gate photo fills the screen.
2. Drama/music atmosphere appears with lights and music-note decorations.
3. Visitor clicks WELCOME / OPEN THE GATE.
4. The two animated gate panels swing outward in 3D.
5. The scene zooms forward.
6. The main modern glassmorphism school portal appears.
